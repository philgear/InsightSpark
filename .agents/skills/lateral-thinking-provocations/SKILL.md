# Lateral Thinking & Provocation Workbench Skill

This skill guides AI agents in generating creative lateral leaps, escaping cognitive valleys, and pairing divergent provocations with grounding execution counterbalances.

---

## 1. The 14 Divergent Provocations

Edward de Bono established that lateral thinking deliberately disrupts pattern-matching habits to uncover non-obvious ideas:

1. **What If? (`what-if`)**: Invert core assumptions or eliminate a ubiquitous axiom.
2. **Redefine Constraints (`constraints`)**: Impose radical scarcity or remove a primary limitation to reveal essential leverage.
3. **The Butterfly Effect (`butterfly`)**: Trace minute micro-interventions that cascade into systemic shifts.
4. **Combinatorial Evolution (`combinatorial`)**: Cross-pollinate two unrelated systems or industries.
5. **Opposite Day (`opposite`)**: Argue the strict inverse of the prevailing consensus.
6. **Future Vision (`future`)**: Leap 10–20 years into the future and reverse-engineer back to the present.
7. **Child's Play (`child`)**: Strip away jargon and reduce the concept to its simplest, most intuitive essence.
8. **Alien Perspective (`alien`)**: View the challenge with zero cultural assumptions or historical baggage.
9. **Nature's Wisdom (`nature`)**: Biomimicry — find how biological evolutionary blueprints solved analogous problems.
10. **Superpower (`superpower`)**: Unshackle from current physics or technological limits, then bridge back to feasibility.
11. **Eliminate & Simplify (`simplify`)**: Ruthlessly delete steps, components, or friction until only the core remains.
12. **Random Object (`random`)**: Introduce a completely orthogonal random concept to force unexpected conceptual bridges.
13. **First Principles (`first-principles`)**: Deconstruct the problem down to irreducible foundational truths.
14. **Root Cause / 5 Whys (`root-cause`)**: Dig sequentially five layers beneath superficial symptoms.

---

## 2. The Grounding Counterbalances (Shelf 2)

Divergent brainstorming without grounding produces fragile novelty. Every creative burst must be anchored by:

* **FMEA (Failure Mode & Effects Analysis - `fmea`)**:
  - Pre-mortem risk assessment.
  - Identification of single points of failure, edge-case bottlenecks, and unintended consequences.
  - Proactive guardrail design.

* **Critical Path Method (`critical-path`)**:
  - Non-negotiable sequence dependencies: $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C$.
  - Identifying the bottleneck that dictates total project completion time.
  - Actionable milestone roadmaps.

---

## 3. Pairing Protocol

When prompting or synthesizing insights:
1. Select 2–3 divergent provocations for disruptive generation.
2. Pair with at least 1 grounding counterbalance (FMEA or Critical Path).
3. Ensure every insight is expressed in concise, concrete, and jargon-free terms.
